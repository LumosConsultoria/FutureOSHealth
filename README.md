# FutureOS Health — AI Healthcare Architecture Concept

**Status:** independent product and architecture concept. Not a deployed clinical product.

This repository documents a visual architecture blueprint for a vendor-neutral clinical intelligence platform. The scope includes interoperability, data pipelines, AI-assisted workflows, human oversight and evaluation gates.

## View
Open `index.html` in a browser (static HTML/CSS/JS; no credentials or backend).

## Scope and provenance
- Product architecture and strategy study, not an operational clinical AI system.
- Illustrative examples and investment scenarios are **not** validated business or clinical outcomes.
- Does not contain customer data, licensed proprietary healthcare software, or employer code.
- Any real-world deployment would require authorization, data governance, regulatory and clinical review.

Healthcare interoperability · enterprise architecture · AI adoption · model evaluation planning · product strategy.
